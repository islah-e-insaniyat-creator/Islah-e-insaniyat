Privacy Policy — Islah-e-Insaniyat

App: Islah-e-Insaniyat (اصلاحِ انسانیت) · Package: com.islaheinsaniyat.app · Effective date: 21 November 2026
Developer: Muhammad Haroon · Contact: harryjutt779@gmail.com

Summary

Islah-e-Insaniyat does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The app works completely offline. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data on your device

Your bookmarks, last reading position and settings (theme, font size, translation on/off) are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access). When you choose to share or copy a verse, hadith or dua, the text is handed to the Android share sheet or clipboard that you control.

External links

The "Social" tab contains links to our YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Channel pages. Tapping one opens that app or your web browser outside this app; those services are governed by their own privacy policies. The app itself sends nothing to them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
